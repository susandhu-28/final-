Procession & Farewell Ceremonies

  • Baraat: The groom's grand procession to the wedding venue, traditionally riding a decorated horse accompanied by dancing relatives and live dhol music.
  • Milni: Formal meeting at the venue entrance where corresponding male elders of both families exchange flower garlands.
  • Vidaai / Doli: The emotional farewell where the bride bids goodbye to her parents, throwing puffed rice over her head as a symbolic blessing of prosperity.
