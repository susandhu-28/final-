1. The Meaning & Spiritual Significance

The term Anand Karaj translates to "Blissful Union" or "Joyful Event." Unlike cultural celebrations that focus on social festivities, the Anand Karaj is a solemn spiritual ceremony in which the bride and groom commit to living as one soul in two bodies, centered around divine wisdom.

2. The Four Sacred Laavan (Vows)

The core of the ceremony involves four circumferential walks around the holy scripture, the Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. Each round represents a distinct stage of spiritual growth and marital commitment:

  • First Laav: Emphasizes duty, family responsibility, and righteous living.
  • Second Laav: Represents the awakening of love and the union of two souls.
  • Third Laav: Symbolizes detachment from worldly distractions and devotion to divine truth.
  • Fourth Laav: Represents spiritual harmony, complete union, and eternal bliss.

3. Gurdwara Etiquette & Seating Protocol

The ceremony takes place inside a Sikh place of worship, known as a Gurdwara. All attendees remove their shoes and cover their heads before entering the sanctuary. Everyone sits together on the carpeted floor as equals before the holy scripture, reflecting the core Sikh principle of Sangat (spiritual congregation and social equality).
