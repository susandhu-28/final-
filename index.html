Traditional Punjabi weddings are vibrant, joyful celebrations filled with rich cultural heritage, sacred rituals, and deep family traditions. From the energetic pre-wedding festivities to the solemn spiritual union of the Anand Karaj, each event carries unique historical and symbolic significance. This interactive site guides you through every step of the journey—exploring key ceremonies, visual layouts, and traditional terminology.
